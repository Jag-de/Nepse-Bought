# Nepse-Bought


It was a time when the new government had just been formed, and market hype was at its peak. One day, a friend living abroad joked, “I heard you’ve bought the entire NEPSE!”

The reality? I was just applying for 10 kittas in one IPO after another.

But his joke got me thinking: what would it actually cost to buy the entire NEPSE? I looked up the total market capitalization and found it was around Rs. 5 trillion; far beyond what an individual could realistically afford.
Then I thought: what if I bought just the minimum units of every listed security? And that question became the inspiration for this data project.

So, shoutout to XYZ—the friend whose joke started it all!
